# PredictRx Sentinel — grant and sponsorship one-pager

## Safer ICU workflow and locally governed AI evaluation in Bangladesh

PredictRx Sentinel is a **retrospective research prototype** that combines an ICU digital flowsheet, structured shift handoff, quality dashboard, and silent sepsis-risk evaluation. It is not approved for clinical use, has not been validated in Bangladesh, and is not offered as a diagnostic or treatment system. The project does not claim improved outcomes, prevented sepsis, reduced mortality, or cost savings.

## The practical problem

ICU teams and quality programmes need reliable longitudinal observations, clear handoff ownership, visible data gaps, and auditable review. Imported prediction models also require local evaluation because measurement practices, populations, prevalence, labels, and workflows can differ by hospital.

## Proposed 8–12 week SafeLaunch

With one Bangladesh hospital partner, the project would:

1. map a minimum ICU dataset and document provenance, units, delays, and missingness;
2. deploy a hospital-controlled digital flowsheet, handoff workflow, and quality dashboard;
3. establish access, audit, backup, retention, incident, and data-closeout controls;
4. evaluate the unchanged frozen Run 4 model retrospectively and, only with approval, silently;
5. train local champions and publish a site-owned go/extend/redesign/stop decision.

Sepsis scores remain hidden from bedside care. Run 4 and its thresholds remain unchanged. No treatment advice, automated action, reinforcement learning, or federated learning is in scope.

## Outputs

- signed governance, data-use, ethics/institutional, security, and evaluation pack;
- reusable source mapping and data-quality report;
- functioning workflow/research pilot in one adult ICU;
- prespecified local report on missingness, calibration, discrimination, simulated alert burden, and feasible subgroups;
- implementation-cost and adoption evidence;
- open, non-sensitive implementation learnings where the hospital permits.

## Measures

Targets are agreed before analysis and reported with limitations:

- mapping coverage, provenance, feed timeliness, quarantines, uptime, and restore test;
- training completion, workflow usage, handoff completeness/time, and user burden;
- prespecified cohort completion and adjudication volume;
- local model performance and simulated burden, without outcome-improvement inference;
- procurement duration, implementation hours, support cost, and continuation decision.

Suggested operational starting points—not validated standards—include 100 encounters, 30 adjudicated outcomes, at least 90% of expected messages after agreed exclusions, and at least 80% of designated users trained.

## Safeguards

- minimum necessary data, least privilege, pseudonymised research exports, and explicit retention/deletion;
- model/data cards, frozen hashes, audit trail, and documented source provenance;
- no bedside score visibility and immediate stop-work triggers for misuse or security events;
- independent hospital clinical, ethics, privacy, legal, and security authority;
- Bangladesh regulatory classification and any DGDA obligations treated as **legal/regulatory review required**, not asserted as settled.

## Funding request structure

The amount should be built from partner-verified costs rather than presented as a fixed fact:

- hospital staff time and clinical adjudication;
- FDE, integration, training, and project management;
- local infrastructure, security review/testing, and backup;
- biostatistics, ethics administration, monitoring, and dissemination;
- accessibility/localization and contingency.

Illustrative discovery range for one 8–12 week site: **BDT 500,000–2,500,000**, highly dependent on hospital in-kind support, interfaces, hardware, security requirements, and research scope. A funder budget must use quotations and eligible-cost rules.
