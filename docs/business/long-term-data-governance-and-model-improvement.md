# Long-term data governance & model improvement

**Default:** hospital-controlled clinical data; purpose-specific authorization; minimization; site-local processing where intended; separate operational metadata; retention/deletion; portability/return; auditability; clinician-reviewed model changes.

**Must not:** automatic online learning; pool hospital patient data across sites without agreements; treat de-identification as unrestricted reuse; claim federated learning as implemented.

Future control workflows (documented, not automatic): site calibration, retrospective evaluation, multi-site evidence, dataset provenance, controlled updates, recalibration/retraining approval, clinical review, rollout/rollback, privacy-preserving research where justified.

Competitive advantage: reusable integrations, site experience, data-quality knowledge, governed evidence, clinician feedback, playbooks, trusted relationships — not unrestricted PHI collection.
