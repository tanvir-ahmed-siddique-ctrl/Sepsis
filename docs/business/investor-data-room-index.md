# PredictRx Sentinel — investor data-room index

## Truth labels

Every artifact should carry one status:

- **Observed:** directly measured or signed evidence.
- **Internal test:** product/model behavior measured by the team.
- **External source:** independently published context.
- **Assumption:** pricing, market, cost or adoption hypothesis to test.
- **Planned:** not complete and not represented as evidence.
